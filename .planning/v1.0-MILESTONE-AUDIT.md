## Executive Summary

Milestone v1.0 hardens the Local Story Archive against silent-failure classes (dead cookies, broken selectors, unbounded memory growth, sanitization gaps). The roadmap defines five sequential phases delivering 27 requirements. **Only Phase 1 is currently complete.** Phase 1 is internally sound — all 6 of its requirements are satisfied, all 4 ROADMAP success criteria verified end-to-end, all wiring intact, anti-pattern and lint scans clean, 220 tests passing — but 21 of 27 milestone requirements are orphaned because Phases 2–5 have not yet been planned or executed.

The audit fails the milestone gate (`gaps_found` is forced by the 21 unsatisfied requirements per the FAIL rule) but contains no Phase-1-internal blockers. The single forward-looking integration concern worth flagging now is `Manifest.connect()` in `archive/state.py:57-68`, which lacks the `check_same_thread=False` + `PRAGMA busy_timeout=5000` configuration that Phase 4's in-story parallelism explicitly depends on (per `STATE.md` decision log).
